Privacy notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

Keystone North Property Partners is run by Jason Nota. We help homeowners sell property quickly and introduce property investors to deals in Leeds and Greater Manchester. We are responsible for the personal information you give us.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Details about a property you want to sell: address, condition, your reasons and timescale.
  • If you are an investor: your budget, preferred areas, strategy and how you plan to fund a purchase.
  • Your answers to the short questions on our website, which help us work out how we can best help you.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a no-obligation offer or property options.
  • To send investors deals that match the criteria they gave us.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.

We use your information because you asked us to contact you, and so we can take steps you have requested before any agreement. We will only send you marketing emails if you have asked for them, and every email lets you opt out.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. If you are selling a property, we only share its details with a matched investor once you agree. We use trusted services to run our business, including Google (email and records), n8n (automation) and Anthropic (AI tools that help us sort enquiries and draft replies). Some of these services may process data outside the UK under recognised safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless a transaction means we must keep them longer, for example anti-money-laundering records, which the law requires us to keep for five years.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, or stop marketing at any time.

Contact us: info@keystonenorth.co.uk

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
