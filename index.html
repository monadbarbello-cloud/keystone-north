About us

We know these streets, these houses and the people in them.

JN
Jason NotaFounder, Keystone North Property Partners

Keystone North was built on years of knocking on doors across the North of England. That's not a line on a website. It means thousands of real conversations on real doorsteps, with people at every stage of their lives, and a feel for what a street is actually worth that you don't get from a spreadsheet.

Today we focus on Leeds and Greater Manchester, two of the strongest rental markets in the country and two places we know street by street.

The job is simple enough. Help homeowners who need a quick, fair sale get one without being messed about. Give investors access to deals that genuinely stack up, with the workings laid out so they can check them. Honest numbers, straight talking, no gimmicks.

Questions

The things people ask us first.

SellersWill you really take it in any condition?
Yes. Damp, dated, unmodernised, fire-damaged, part-built, tenanted or empty for years. The condition changes the figure, not whether we're interested.
SellersIs there any cost to me?
None. The valuation and the offer are free, there's no obligation to accept, and we don't charge homeowners a fee at any point.
SellersWill I get the full market price?
Usually not, and we won't pretend otherwise. You're trading a slice of the headline price for speed and certainty. If you've got six months and a property in good order, the open market may well serve you better, and we'll say so.
InvestorsHow are the deals priced?
Every pack shows the purchase price, refurbishment estimate, comparable sold prices and expected rent. Our sourcing fee is stated clearly on the pack and is payable on reservation.
InvestorsCan I invest remotely?
Yes. The packs are built so you can assess a deal properly from anywhere, and we handle viewings, access and progress on the ground.
BothWhich areas do you cover?
Leeds and Greater Manchester are our core patch, and we'll look at the surrounding towns where the numbers work. If a property sits outside that, we'll tell you straight away rather than waste your time.

Not sure which side you're on?

Plenty of people come to us owning one property and leave thinking like an investor, or the other way round. Either way, start with a conversation and no obligation.
